# The aesthetics

One per template. Paste into the `AESTHETIC` block in PROMPT.md.

Ordered by what competitor research says sells. **Build 1–3 first.**

---

### 1. Mughal Zamarrud

**Palette:** deep emerald green with antique gold
**Scene:** a Mughal palace gateway at dusk — carved marble jali screens, an
emerald-inlaid arch, brass lanterns, a still reflecting pool
**Feeling:** opulent, candlelit, hushed

### 2. Kesari Mandap

**Palette:** marigold and saffron with deep red
**Scene:** a wedding mandap at golden hour — thick marigold garlands, carved
pillars wrapped in banana leaf, brass urns, petals scattered on warm stone
**Feeling:** festive, sunlit, alive

### 3. Kanjeevaram

**Palette:** maroon silk with gold zari
**Scene:** a South Indian temple courtyard at dawn — a carved gopuram, strung
jasmine, rows of brass oil lamps, a kolam drawn on the floor
**Feeling:** sacred, warm, unhurried

### 4. Royal Jaipur

**Palette:** crimson and gold
**Scene:** a Jaipur palace gate — carved sandstone arches, painted floral
frescoes, live peacocks either side, brass lanterns, marigold bowls, light
reflecting off a polished marble floor
**Feeling:** regal, grand, cinematic

### 5. Midnight Mayura

**Palette:** peacock navy and teal with gold
**Scene:** a palace terrace at night — latticed balconies, lotus pools under
moonlight, peacock feathers, hanging lamps
**Feeling:** cool, jewelled, still

### 6. Noor-e-Nikah

**Palette:** ivory, emerald and gold
**Scene:** a mosque courtyard at evening — a scalloped arch, Arabic
calligraphy carved into stone, hanging brass lamps, white roses
**Feeling:** serene, luminous, reverent

### 7. Blush Garden

**Palette:** blush pink and rose gold with sage
**Scene:** an English garden at early morning — climbing roses, pale drapes
on a pergola, dew, soft diffused light
**Feeling:** romantic, airy, gentle

### 8. Haveli Bandhej

**Palette:** terracotta, rose and indigo
**Scene:** a Rajasthani haveli courtyard — jharokha windows, bandhani cloth
hung to dry, Jaipur blue pottery, a painted elephant motif on the wall
**Feeling:** handmade, sun-baked, characterful

### 9. Phulkari Anand

**Palette:** saffron and ivory with gold
**Scene:** a gurdwara courtyard at sunrise — phulkari embroidered cloth,
marigold strings, a canopy in soft morning light
**Feeling:** bright, devotional, warm

### 10. Monochrome Vow

**Palette:** ivory and charcoal
**Scene:** a modern stone colonnade — white florals, deep shadow, raking
light, nothing ornate
**Feeling:** editorial, restrained, expensive
