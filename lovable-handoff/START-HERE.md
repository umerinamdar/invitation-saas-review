# Build premium wedding invitation templates

This folder tells Lovable exactly what to build and what to hand back.

## How to use it

1. Open **PROMPT.md**
2. Pick one aesthetic from **AESTHETICS.md**
3. Paste the prompt into a **new Lovable project**, with that aesthetic
   pasted into the `AESTHETIC` block
4. When it finishes, export the project and send it back
5. Repeat per template — **one Lovable project each**, so the artwork
   stays separate

Start with **two or three**. Get those integrated and judged before
spending time on ten.

## The bar

The benchmark is invitima.com. Their templates open on a **photoreal painted
Rajasthani palace gate** — carved sandstone arches, live peacocks, brass
lanterns, marigold bowls, light reflecting off a marble floor — with the
couple's names in gold script beneath and a "TAP TO OPEN" plate on the door.

That is the standard. Not a coloured background with a border: a painted
scene with depth, warmth and detail.

**If the artwork is not at that level, the template is not finished.** The
generated images are the whole value here — the motion and the structure
already exist on our side.

## What comes back to us

Best: the whole project export.

Minimum, per template:

- The **four image files** (see PROMPT.md for sizes)
- The **palette as hex values**, labelled by role
- The **two Google Font names**

We do not need Lovable's code. We have the engine — the envelope and door
animations, the audio fade, the scratch card, the countdown. What we cannot
make is the artwork.

## One hard rule

**The wax seal must be blank.** No letters, no initials, no monogram.

Our engine prints each couple's own initials onto it. A seal with letters
baked in means every customer receives somebody else's monogram — we have
already had that bug once.
